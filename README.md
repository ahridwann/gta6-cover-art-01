Porto Solmar Cover Studio turns your own photos into a GTA-style poster cover for the fictional city of Porto Solmar. Pick one of four shots — Skyline Dusk, Neon Street, Beach Sunset or Downtown Night — fill one, two or three panels with photos from your machine, crop and position each one, write the big block-letter title and a script subtitle, and export a share-ready PNG in square (1080 × 1080), portrait (1080 × 1350) or wide (1920 × 1080). Everything runs offline from static files: photos are read locally and never uploaded, the skyline and sunset grade are painted on canvas, and the fonts ship inside the app under the SIL Open Font License. Undo and redo keep every step, and four ready-made cover recipes live in app/shots.json.

## Run it

```sh
node serve.ts
```

How it works

  1. Choose one of the four Porto Solmar shots — each is a different time of day in the city.
  2. Add a photo to each panel, then drag zoom and position until the crop feels right.
  3. Write the title and caption in your own words.
  4. Export a PNG in square, portrait or wide — ready to post.
